Privacy.

How this website handles your contact details and whom to ask about your privacy.

Who can you contact?

Vleesbedrijf P. Wils N.V., Bouwelven 15, 2280 Grobbendonk, Belgium, is responsible for processing the personal data you provide to Wils.

If you have a question about your personal data, email info@vleesbedrijf-wils.be and mention that your enquiry concerns privacy.

Your enquiry

The form asks for your name, email address and message. Your company name and telephone number are optional. For a product enquiry, you can also add products and specify your requirements, packaging, whether you need fresh or frozen products, quantity and timing.

Wils uses the information in your message to handle your enquiry and respond. Include only the information needed to deal with your enquiry.

Select Compose email to open the full draft in your email application. Review it there and send it yourself to the department shown. Sending takes place in your email application. You can also copy the message or save it as a text file on your device.

What stays in your browser?

Your entries stay in the open form when you change topics. Your email draft includes only the fields relevant to the selected topic.

The form does not save your entries to local browser storage or send them to a form service. Your browser may restore the fields when you return to the page. When you switch languages, the website uses temporary session storage in this browser tab to carry over your form entries. The entries can be restored for up to one minute and are removed after restoration. Once sent, your message is processed in your email system and the recipient’s, as well as by the email services involved.

The website uses temporary session storage for page transitions. It stores page addresses and technical information about the transition. Form entries are excluded. The site reads and removes the stored entry at the next page transition.

Website and external links

When your browser loads pages, images and other files, the web server receives technical information such as your IP address and the requested file. Contact Wils if you have questions about how this data is processed.

The map is an image served by this website. Google Maps opens only when you select the map or Get directions. Industry links and some document links may also take you to other websites, where the provider’s own privacy rules apply.

Your rights

Subject to the conditions set out in data protection law, you can ask to access your personal data, have inaccurate data corrected, and request erasure or restriction of processing. In certain cases, you also have the right to data portability or to object to processing.

If processing is based on your consent, you can withdraw that consent. This does not affect the lawfulness of any processing carried out before you withdrew it.

To exercise a right or ask how Wils keeps and handles your message, email info@vleesbedrijf-wils.be.

Questions or complaints

Contact Wils with any question or complaint about your personal data. You can also lodge a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority (opens in a new tab).
