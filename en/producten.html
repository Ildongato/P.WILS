Custom specifications and packaging.

We tailor the specification, fat content and packaging to your production requirements.

Cut and composition

We can discuss a different composition, for example with or without rind, with the nasal bone, or with or without the inner ear.

Fat content

We can discuss your target fat content or your own product specification.

Packaging

We use poly-wrapped blocks, unwrapped blocks and cardboard boxes. Other packaging is agreed in advance.

Fresh or frozen

We supply fresh or frozen pork.
